The AllInsight **code** is free software under GPL-3.0-or-later. You may use, study, change and share it under that license.

The AllInsight **name, logo and app icon** are not covered by the GPL. They identify official builds, so people can trust that something called AllInsight came from this project.

## You may, without asking

- Say your software works with, or is based on, AllInsight.
- Use the name in articles, reviews, tutorials and videos about AllInsight.
- Redistribute **unmodified** official releases under the AllInsight name, as long as you don't bundle other software with them.
- Use the logo to link to this project, without altering it.

## You may not

- Distribute a **modified** version under the AllInsight name or logo. Forks must use a different name and icon.
- Bundle AllInsight with adware, toolbars, "optimizers", or any other software, or wrap it in a downloader.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests we endorse your product, service or company.
- Use a confusingly similar name, logo or domain.
- Register domains, app store listings or social accounts that imitate the project.

## Why this matters

PC utilities are often repackaged with adware. This policy lets anyone reuse the code while making sure the AllInsight name always means a clean, unmodified build.
