Cleanup that never guesses.

Before anything can be removed, every file AllInsight finds is sorted into one of three groups. Only one of them can ever be cleaned automatically.

Data Windows and your apps rebuild on their own

Turn on Auto-Clean and AllInsight clears these when free space drops below the level you choose. It always shows you the total first.

  • Windows temporary files
  • Application caches
  • Crash dumps
  • Thumbnail cache
  • Shader caches
  • Old update caches
  • Error reports
  • Recycle Bin, after you confirm

Things only you can judge

AllInsight lists them with size, location and date, and leaves the decision to you. Anything you remove goes to the Recycle Bin.

  • Old downloads
  • Very large files
  • Duplicate files
  • Old installers
  • ZIP and RAR archives
  • ISO images
  • Large videos
  • Unused app data

Your work, your system, and anything unknown

These are excluded from every cleanup, automatic or manual. You can add your own folders to this list.

  • Documents and Desktop
  • Pictures, Videos, Music
  • Project folders and source code
  • Databases
  • Browser profiles
  • App settings
  • Windows and Program Files
  • Unknown files

Questions

Will AllInsight delete my files?

Not without you. Automatic cleanup only touches the Safe group: temporary files and caches that Windows and your apps rebuild. Documents, photos, downloads and anything unknown are never removed automatically, and anything you choose to delete goes to the Recycle Bin when it can be restored.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Every feature, including the AI assistant, runs offline. If AllInsight ever adds an online feature, it will be off until you turn it on.

What does the AI actually see?

A summary AllInsight prepares: folder sizes, file counts, drive readings and startup items. It never reads the contents of your files and it can't run commands or delete anything.

Do I need a powerful laptop for the AI?

No. AllInsight suggests a model that fits your memory, and it runs on an ordinary processor. If you'd rather not use AI at all, skip the model and every other feature still works.

What if my drive doesn't report health data?

AllInsight shows the status as Unknown and lists which readings are missing. It won't call a drive healthy without the data to back it up.

Why does it sometimes ask for administrator permission?

Some Windows folders can only be measured with elevated access. AllInsight explains what it needs and why before asking, and never asks on every launch.
