Layer one

Deterministic insights.

The device score, its reasons, the recommendations and the Overview summary are all filled in from measurements, by rules that can be read and tested. This is what makes “works fully without a model” true rather than aspirational.

Layer two

A local model, if you want one.

Optional and conversational. It runs as a separate llama.cpp process on your machine, so a model that hangs or runs out of memory cannot take the window with it, and AllInsight itself contains no model weights.

Transparency

Exactly what the model is given.

One block of text, built from measurements, shown to you verbatim. No file names, no file contents, no paths beyond the folders shown. Your question follows an explicit marker, so a question cannot pass itself off as data.

What it cannot do

It returns words.
Nothing else happens.

No part of AllInsight reads the model’s answer for commands, paths or file names. Its output is cleaned of hidden control characters and capped in length. The engine starts without a shell and listens only on 127.0.0.1. There is simply no code path from what the model says to anything on your disk.

Choosing a model

Sized to your disk and your memory.

AllInsight picks the largest model that fits both: at most half your memory, and your free space minus a 6 GB reserve, so installing a model can never be what fills the drive. If nothing fits, it says so.
