Privacy

Nothing leaves
your computer.

Not as a setting. As an absence of code. AllInsight has no account, no update checker, no analytics and no cloud service.

Disconnect the machine and every screen behaves exactly the same. AllInsight shows “Offline mode, all local features available” rather than an error, because offline is not an error. It is the design.

Private because of
what is not there.

No route out

No way to reach the internet.

The only network code in the app is compiled without encryption support and only ever pointed at the computer itself.

No update checker

It never phones home.

Not even to ask whether a new version exists. You decide when to update, from this site.

Permissions

An ordinary user, by default.

AllInsight never asks for administrator rights at launch. Cleanup of your own files, the storage map, duplicate detection, process management and per-user startup entries all work without elevation.

On Linux it never runs as root at all. Everything it cleans belongs to you, and drive health comes from UDisks2 without privilege.

Three exceptions on Windows
Drive wear on SATA drivesWindows only lets an elevated process read those counters. NVMe drives report without it.
Windows Update cleanupThose folders belong to the system.
Machine-wide startup entriesThey live in the machine's part of the registry.

Each one says so where it matters. Without elevation, drive health reads Unknown, with an explanation. Never a guess.
